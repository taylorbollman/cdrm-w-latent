The ordinary-autograd FP32 CDRM reference is implemented and has passed the declared correctness and operational checks. The current research comparison is **SEQ6 versus CDRM6 on official MAD selective copying, T256, vocabulary 16, 96 copied tokens, seeds 0 and 1**. This is a draft from completed evidence: both seed pairs have completed and been audited at epoch 25. The seed-0 pair has also completed and been audited at the selected epoch 45. CDRM seed 1 is the final active continuation; its epoch-45 pair, the controller's full-cohort completion, final-set evaluations and complete session retention remain pending. Partial continuation metrics are excluded, and unequal per-seed endpoints are not averaged.

The implementation preserves an ordinary backbone and adds one causal side scan. In the selected six-block profile, ordinary blocks 0–3 produce the post-block-1 early state and post-block-3 late state. The scan shares block 1's canonical attention, learned normalization and MLP parameters through differentiable fused-QKV slices; it registers only two separate, nonzero, bias-free D-to-D adapters. Each read sees earlier permanent records and the current temporary K/V pair, and its permanent write becomes visible on the next token. The deep candidate is `p_early + 0.1 Ad(Nd(p_late-p_early))`. Rho 1 stores the proposed state; the bridge is `p_late + 0.01 Ab(Nb(hat_m-p_early))`, followed by ordinary blocks 4–5. Memory remains connected to autograd and is rebuilt per example/forward. The [implementation and independent review](code-review.md), [resolved profiles](../../../configs/cdrm/README.md), and [usage guide](../../cdrm-naive-usage.md) document the exact contract.

The active same-depth control instead feeds `p_early` into the candidate adapter while retaining the late bridge anchor. It is implemented and tested, but its research arm is deferred. Replacing the late preview with the early preview would zero the difference input and is a separate removal ablation. Lambda zero is the matched SEQ bypass; rho zero leaves an active side branch. CDRM is disabled by default and adds no parameters in that path. Existing SEQ/R3 and BF16 defaults remain intact. A wrapper-only compatibility fix preserves the legacy HF tuple fields when native output gains optional CDRM diagnostics.

The selected run is D128/H16/MLP512, six blocks, sites 1/3, physical B128, FP32 parameters/computation, math attention, autocast/TF32/dropout off and no accumulation. AdamW uses LR `5e-4`, betas `(0.9,0.98)`, epsilon `1e-8`, weight decay zero and clip norm one. The cosine scheduler steps after complete epochs over a fixed 200-epoch horizon, without warmup, to minimum LR `1e-6`. Stopping at 25 does not compress that schedule. Fixed train/dev splits and shuffle seed 45678 are shared across architectures and seeds. Each CDRM starts from the corresponding fresh SEQ backbone initialization; the trained SEQ screen supplies its baseline, not its initialization.

The [protocol audit](protocol-alignment.md) distinguishes this adaptation from a paper reproduction. Per-block D128/MLP512/H16 and ALiBi align with the cited configuration; six-block depth, the added side scan, the local precision/normalization/head choices and explicit scheduler stepping do not establish reproduction of the published experiment. One LR candidate is used without the paper's tuning sweep, and 25 epochs is a partial run. The fixed data counts and B128 are MAD defaults, not verified settings for the authors' plotted runs. Same-depth and R3 learning controls, a complete hyperparameter sweep and broad seed uncertainty are outside this comparison.
